========================================================================
GitHub Contributions Desktop Widget for Windows
========================================================================

How to Use:
1. Double-click "GitHubDesktopWidget.exe" to run.
2. Enter your GitHub username when prompted.
3. The widget will stay fixed directly on your Windows desktop!

Features:
- Fixed to Home Desktop: Sits quietly on your wallpaper behind your open apps.
- Resizable: Grab any edge or corner to resize the widget to your liking.
- 30D Compact Mode: Clicking "30D" automatically shrinks the widget to a compact size.
- Eye Pointer: A mini companion eye tracks your mouse cursor across the widget.
- Daily Hover Details: Hover over any circular dot to view the exact contribution count and date.
- Liquid Glass: Transparent glass background with a subtle emerald glow border.
- Right-Click Menu: Access Settings, Themes (Liquid Glass, Classic, OLED, Aurora), Startup, and Pin controls.
